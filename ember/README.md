# Ember

An AI agent that has to earn more than its API calls cost.

Ember runs on your Home Assistant server, pays for every model call from a
balance you fund, and looks for honest ways to earn money. You approve anything
it wants to do in the outside world and you record its revenue. It can run out
of money and die, and that is an acceptable outcome.

Dashboard through Ingress, hard spending caps enforced in code, dry-run mode
with a fake model so you can try everything without spending money. Ember makes
finished products itself: PDF documents with an editable Word copy, Excel
spreadsheets and listing photos. For anything else it has a workshop: code
written and run in Anthropic's sandbox, and a script that proves useful becomes
an upgrade request, so Ember grows new abilities. With your Etsy shop connected,
Ember lists the products you approve there itself. A share of its spending goes
to ventures: a growing tree of new ways to earn, which Ember brainstorms,
scores and researches, bringing you business cases to back, park or kill. Optionally Ember gets its own
mailbox: it reads its mail on its own and sends an email only after you approve
it.
